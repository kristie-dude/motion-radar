How to read it. Motion gives every creator 1 to 8 stars based on the last 14 days, and lineups have a star cap. Stars and scores aren't public, so this uses rank instead. Avg rank is the creator's average position over the saved history (up to 14 days). A high number there means a cold recent past, which likely means fewer stars. Heat is how much better their rank is now than that average. Cheap & rising = bottom half on avg rank, and up 2 or more spots. It's an estimate, not official star data.
Fan-made for Mogathon. Not affiliated with Motion. Data: leaderboard.motion.game, saved every 4 hours since 13 Nov 2026 in this repo. The leaderboard's own change number is the move over the last 4 hours.
