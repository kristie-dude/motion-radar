        Avg rank is the creator's average position over the saved history, up to 14 days, the same window Motion uses for stars. Heat is how many spots better they rank now than that average. Stars aren't public, so cheap is an estimate. The ring around each avatar shows the move in the selected window: green for climbing, orange for falling. Select a row to open its chart.

        Indicators

        Rank #1 is at the top, so a rising line means climbing. There's one point every 4 hours, so 6 points is a day. Star baseline is the creator's average rank over the last 14 days, the same window Motion uses for stars: a rank line well above it means they're hot now and their stars probably haven't caught up. Crossover compares a fast and a slow average (6 and 18 updates, one day and three days) for the first creator, and marks where momentum turns. Momentum shows spots gained or lost at each update.
