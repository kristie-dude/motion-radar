        Rank charts

        On the lines
        Lower panel

        Rank #1 is at the top, so a rising line means climbing. There's one point every 4 hours. Every indicator is drawn for each creator in that creator's colour, so they work the same with one creator or four. The line breaks wherever a creator was off the leaderboard.

        Star baseline
        Average position over the last 14 days, the window Motion uses for stars. A rank line above it means the creator is doing better than their stars suggest. It's measured as a share of the board, so it stays fair if the leaderboard grows.
        Trend
        The rank line smoothed over the last few updates (6 is one day), so the direction shows through the noise.
        Turns
        Arrows where the one-day average crosses the three-day average. Green means the creator started climbing, orange means they started falling.
        Heat
        Spots above (+) or below (−) their own 14-day average right now. High heat means hot now while the stars still reflect the past.
        24h change
        Spots gained (+) or lost (−) over the previous 24 hours, at every point. Easy to compare between creators.
