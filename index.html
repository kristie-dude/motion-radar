Every move on the Motion creator leaderboard, saved every four hours. Find who's climbing before their stars catch up.

Who's moving

Each dot is a creator. Hover a dot to see who it is, or select it to open their chart. Higher means they climbed more places in the selected window. Further right means a lower average rank over the last 14 days, which usually means fewer stars. Creators with less than a day on the board are marked New and kept out of the pink corner. The faint trails show where each dot was over the last three updates (12 hours), so you can tell who is speeding up and who is slowing down. The Trails button turns them off.
