All creators

How to read it. Motion gives every creator 1 to 8 stars based on the last 14 days, and lineups have a star cap. Stars and scores aren't public, so this uses rank instead. Avg rank is the creator's average position over the saved history (up to 14 days). A high number there means a cold recent past, which likely means fewer stars. Heat is how much better their rank is now than that average. Cheap & rising = bottom half on avg rank, and up 2 or more spots. It's an estimate, not official star data. Click any name to open its chart.
Chart Candle Indicators
How to read it. The line is the creator's rank on the Motion leaderboard over time, with #1 at the top, so up means climbing. One point every 4 hours. Indicators count in points, so SMA 6 is a 24-hour average. Candles group a period into open, close, best and worst rank; green means they climbed over that candle. Momentum shows spots gained or lost at each update for the first creator you picked. Bollinger and momentum follow the first creator; SMA and EMA are drawn for everyone. History starts on 13 Nov 2026, when saving began.
Fan-made for Mogathon. Not affiliated with Motion. Data: leaderboard.motion.game, saved every 4 hours since 13 Nov 2026 in this repo. The leaderboard's own change number is the move over the last 4 hours. Charts by TradingView Lightweight Charts.
